## Instructions for apps

Overview of the applications currently in TuyuOS

## TUYU
A biography app designed to introduce the main inspiration behind TuyuOS

It provides a brief overview of the Japanese music unit **TUYU**, including their background, discography, and legacy that inspired the coooool style and atmosphere of this desktop environment

Features a scrollable page covering the band's background, discography, and more. Scroll down to explore the full content. (Please dont miss it)

## Weather
A live weather app powered by [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) and [Nominatim](https://nominatim.org/).

Dedicated to give an immersive experience when it comes to the rainy aesthetic of TUYU.

- Requests your location on first launch
- Allow it to display:
  - Current temperature
  - Humidity
  - Wind speed
  - Weather condition
The dynamic background changes based on the live weather, serving as a reference to TUYU song titles and MVs

For testing, weather moods can be previewed using the developer console via
  testMood("")

Valid moods:
  - "Summer Breeze"
  - "Overcast Skies"
  - "Hide and Seek Alone"
  - "It's Raining After All"
  - "Heavy Rain Fall"
  - "When Morning Glory Falls"
